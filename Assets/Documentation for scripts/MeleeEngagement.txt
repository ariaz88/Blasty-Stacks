PURPOSE
-------
Defines WHERE a melee unit has to stand to be able to hit its target, and how to
tell whether it is standing there yet. One rule, shared by heroes and enemies,
so the two factions cannot hold different ideas of "close enough".

Added 2026-09-11 to fix a reported bug: when an enemy and a hero lined up
vertically, neither could damage the other, they sank into each other's sprites,
and the enemy visibly slid forward into the hero's body.

WHY IT IS NEEDED
----------------
Every melee weapon in the project is a WIDE, FLAT trigger on a hand or weapon
bone. Player_Valkyrie's sword box is 7.68 x 1.29 local units offset +4.11 on X;
at the visual's 0.19 scale that is ~1.46 wide by only ~0.24 TALL, reaching
SIDEWAYS from its owner. Enemy weapon boxes have the same shape (Enemy_Orc:
6.58 x 1.19 offset +2.79).

A box like that can only touch something standing BESIDE its owner. Two units
stacked vertically - the natural result of enemies marching straight down a lane
into heroes walking straight up it - are inside each other's attack RANGE while
being completely outside each other's attack BOX. Before this, both sides decided
"I have arrived" from a plain radial distance:

    PlayerPursueTargetState : distance <= pm.maxAttackRange        (0.85)
    EnemyLocoMotion         : distance <= stoppingDistance         (0.83)

Radial distance cannot tell "beside" from "on top of", so a unit approaching from
directly below stopped directly below: in range, unable to hit, sprites merged.

CONSTANTS
---------
  StandoffFactor      0.85   fraction of range to come to rest at. MUST stay
                             below 1 - see the invariant below.
  MinStandoff         0.2    floor, for a unit authored with a tiny range
  BandFactor          0.3    half-height of the strip around the target's own Y
                             where a hit can still land, as a fraction of range
  MinBand             0.1    floor for that band, in world units
  SideDeadZoneX       0.25   below this |dx| a unit cannot read its own position
                             as "I am on this side"; the faction preference wins
  ArrivalSlack        0.8    a unit closer than standoff * this has sunk INTO its
                             target and is sent back out
  PlayerPreferredSide -1     heroes settle on the target's LEFT when dead level
  EnemyPreferredSide  +1     enemies settle on its RIGHT - opposite, ON PURPOSE

  Resolved values for the live prefabs:
    hero  (range 0.85): standoff 0.723, band 0.255, min accepted distance 0.578
    orc   (range 0.83): standoff 0.706, band 0.249, min accepted distance 0.565

METHODS
-------
  Standoff(range)                 -> max(MinStandoff, range * StandoffFactor)
  Band(range)                     -> max(MinBand, range * BandFactor)

  ChooseSide(self, target, preferredSide, heldSide) -> +1 (target's right) / -1
      1. If |self.x - target.x| >= SideDeadZoneX, return the side the unit is
         ALREADY on. This is the whole stability argument: A is on the opposite
         side of B from where B is of A, so both halves of a fighting pair derive
         the SAME arrangement and converge on it.
      2. Inside the dead zone, keep heldSide if there is one (so a unit walking
         round its target does not reverse when it crosses the centre line).
      3. Otherwise take the faction preference. The two are deliberately
         opposite - see NOTES.

  StandPoint(target, range, side, slot) -> Vector2
      The exact point to walk to. Always on the standoff circle, so
      |point - target| == Standoff(range) for every slot.
      slot fans attackers UP AND DOWN the same flank:
         slot 0 -> dy 0, slot 1 -> +band, slot 2 -> -band,
         slot 3 -> +band/2, slot 4 -> -band/2, ...
      THE SLOT MUST NOT FLIP THE SIDE - see NOTES.

  InAttackPosition(self, target, range) -> bool
      TRUE when all three hold:
         distance <= range                    (close enough)
         distance >= Standoff(range) * 0.8     (not sunk into the target)
         |dy| <= Band(range)                   (level enough to connect)
      Every StandPoint satisfies all three with margin.

THE INVARIANT - do not break it
-------------------------------
StandPoint must ALWAYS satisfy InAttackPosition. The movers walk to StandPoint
while the state machines decide with InAttackPosition. If the two can disagree,
the mover reports "arrived" and stops while the state reports "not there yet" and
keeps pursuing, and the unit freezes on the spot playing its walk animation. That
has happened before in this project (PlayerManager's old flat +1.80 sideways slot
put a hero 2.45 from an enemy with a range of 0.85).

HOW IT CONNECTS
---------------
  - PlayerManager.ResolveAttackDestination  - where a hero walks
  - PlayerManager.IsInAttackPosition        - read by PlayerPursueTargetState
                                              and PlayerCombatState
  - EnemyLocoMotion.ResolveStandPoint       - where an enemy walks
  - EnemyLocoMotion.IsInAttackPosition      - read by EnemyManager.HandleCurrentAction
  - MeleeContactRecovery                    - its retry spots come from StandPoint
  - AttackSlotRegistry                      - hands out the slot indices

NOTES
-----
  - GIVING BOTH FACTIONS THE SAME PREFERRED SIDE IS THE ONE THING THAT MUST NEVER
    BE DONE. Both would want to stand on their target's right, two demands with no
    solution, and the pair would walk sideways together for the whole fight.
  - The same trap is why slot no longer mirrors the side. It was tried: a
    simulated 2-heroes-vs-1-enemy fight drifted 9 world units off the lane in 12
    seconds and never landed a hit, because the slot-1 hero wanted the enemy's far
    side while the enemy wanted that hero's far side.
  - Attackers who genuinely approach from opposite sides get opposite sides for
    free, from their own positions - no slot needed.
  - Verified by simulation in the Editor (2026-09-11): 1v1 stacked, 2v1 split
    lanes, 2v1 same side, 3v3 and 5v5 all settle with zero movement in the last
    two seconds, every hero in a position it can hit from, and no lateral drift.
  - A unit still stops the moment InAttackPosition turns true, which is usually
    just BEFORE it reaches its exact stand point - in practice pairs settle around
    |dy| 0.2 rather than 0. That is inside the band, so hits land.
