PURPOSE
-------
Holds the three baked card sprites HeroCardRevealDirector draws with, so the
director can find them IN A BUILD.

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
HeroCardRevealDirector is self-bootstrapping (no scene object references it), so
nothing could hand it sprites. It loaded them with UnityEditor.AssetDatabase,
which does not exist in a player build: every build fell back to a blank white
texture. On device the reward cards were white rectangles, the face-down "?"
back was a blank white card and the teal glow a solid cyan block (device video
2026-10-07, 0:46-0:55). The Editor never showed it.

FIELDS
------
  back   Assets/Arts/VFX/HeroCardBack.png   face-down card with the "?"
  face   Assets/Arts/VFX/HeroCardFace.png   face-up frame under the portrait
  glow   Assets/Arts/VFX/HeroCardGlow.png   halo, baked with padding

  ResourcePath = "VFX/HeroCardSprites"; Load() caches Resources.Load.

NOTES
-----
  - The PNGs stay in Arts/VFX; the asset in Resources only REFERENCES them, which
    is enough to pull them into the build.
  - If the asset is deleted the director logs an error and draws white
    placeholders - the old symptom. Re-create it (Create > Blasty > VFX > Hero
    Card Sprite Set) at the same path and assign the three sprites.
