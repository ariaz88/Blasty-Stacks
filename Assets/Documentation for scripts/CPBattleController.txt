PURPOSE
Stages 1-5 referee: reference CP calibration plus the guaranteed-win model.
Added at runtime by EnemySpawner. Never ticks HP, kills units remotely, stops
waves, or destroys a gate - only real weapon hits ever deal damage.

THE MODEL  (Arash, 2026-09-11)
Exactly ONE hero is assisted: the strongest on the field, chosen when the battle
is prepared. It carries the guarantee alone. Every other hero fights with NO
assistance at all - it wins, loses or dies exactly as it would with this component
absent, and the damage it deals on the way is real. That is what makes the fight
read as genuine rather than staged.

The protected hero is not invulnerable and has no blanket HP floor. Each
INDIVIDUAL enemy instead gets a lifetime allowance of how much of that hero it may
ever remove, spread over at least four hits.

FIELDS
  Instance / IsPrepared        scene-scoped singleton and readiness flag
  PlayerCP / EnemyCP           reference totals for this battle
  PlayerShouldWin              matches cleared vs LevelBattleRules.FirstWinningMatch
  BattleResult / Level / HeroCount
  Champion                     the protected hero; null when the player should lose
  HeroDeaths / EnemyDeaths / FewestHitsBeforeDeath
                               telemetry read by the Editor verification harness
  teamFactor                   team normalisation factor, reused by reinforcements
  budgetPerEnemy / perHitCap   the guarantee, from LevelBattleRules
  plannedEnemyCount            enemies this stage fields (= level number)
  tutorialExchange             levels 1-3: the scripted presentation is active
  spentByEnemy                 per-attacker lifetime damage against the champion

FLOW
  Prepare(spawner, waves)
    1. Resolve level; bail unless stages 1-5 and deployments have settled.
    2. TEAM-TOTAL normalisation. Sum the roster's raw CP, then scale every hero by
       one shared factor so the TOTAL lands on heroCount x PerHeroCP. Preserves
       each hero's share, so a genuinely strongest hero exists - see NOTES.
    3. Enemy budget, EnemyScale, PlayerShouldWin, and the assert that reference CP
       and the deployment outcome agree.
    4. ChooseChampion.

  ChooseChampion(roster)
    Highest CP after normalisation, instance-id tie-break so the choice does not
    depend on spawn order. Returns without choosing when the player is meant to
    LOSE: a lost battle runs completely unassisted, which is what lets a Slow Loss
    earn its length from near-matched armies rather than a damage multiplier.

  RegisterEnemy(enemy)
    Per-unit CP where LevelBattleRules authored it (levels 1-3), otherwise the team
    budget shared out in proportion to each type's authored strength. It no longer
    touches moveSpeed - the levels 1-3 slow-down was removed on 2026-09-12 in
    favour of one uniform authored speed.

  IsTutorialExchange                                                    [static]
    TRUE while levels 1-3 run their scripted exchange. CharacterStats reads it so
    those levels use a FLAT eight blows for every unit instead of the 8-11 defence
    band, which is what levels 4-5 use.

  CalibrateHero(hero, level)                                            [static]
    Ensures MeleeContactRecovery, and applies teamFactor to a hero that arrived
    AFTER Prepare (a mid-battle reinforcement). Heroes present at Prepare are
    scaled there instead, in one pass, because team normalisation needs to see the
    whole roster at once.

  AdjustIncomingDamage(target, damage, attacker)                        [static]
    Called only when a weapon actually hits. `attacker` is REQUIRED by the model:
    budgets are per individual enemy, so identical blows from two enemies have to
    be accounted separately. Order: count the hit; ScriptTutorialBlow in levels
    1-3; then LimitDamageToChampion for the protected hero.

    THE FOUR-HIT CEILING IS NO LONGER HERE. It was, and it did not hold in play -
    a level 4 enemy still died in two hits, because every early return above skips
    it silently (no battle prepared, unit missing from the registered sets, stale
    static Instance, scene mismatch). It now lives in
    CharacterStats.ClampIncomingBlow, which PlayerStats/EnemyStats apply AFTER
    this call, where nothing can bypass it. Do not re-add it here.

  CountEngagedFlankers(out left, out right)                             [private]
    Counts the enemies genuinely fighting the champion on EACH side of it.
    BEING NEARBY IS NOT ENOUGH - an enemy counts only if ALL of these hold:
      1. alive;
      2. within LevelBattleRules.FlankEngageRange (1.2)   - cheap reject first;
      3. clearly to one side, |dx| >= MeleeEngagement.SideDeadZoneX (0.25), so
         directly above or below counts as NEITHER;
      4. its OWN currentTarget is this hero - it is fighting IT, not some other
         hero it happens to be standing beside;
      5. IsInAttackPosition() - close and level enough that its weapon really lands.
    Two enemies standing either side while one of them is busy with somebody else
    is not a flank: the hero only takes free hits when both are hitting IT.
    IT COUNTS RATHER THAN STOPPING AT ONE PER SIDE. The earlier version returned as
    soon as it had a left and a right, which is all a yes/no flank needs - but it
    cannot tell 1-and-1 from 1-and-2, and since 2026-09-12 those are different
    rules. Bounded by the stage's enemy count, 5 at most.

  ChampionIsFlanked                                                    [property]
    left > 0 && right > 0. Public, for on-screen debugging.

  ChampionIsSurrounded                                                 [property]
    Flanked AND left + right >= LevelBattleRules.SurroundedEnemyCount (3) - the
    hero is not merely caught between two but PILED ON, one enemy on one side and
    two on the other. Reported from a level 3 playthrough (Arash, 2026-09-12).
    Public, for on-screen debugging.

  LimitDamageToChampion(attacker, damage)
    0. FLANK HALVING. While flanked, the blow AND the cap are both multiplied by
       LevelBattleRules.FlankedDamageScale (0.5) - level 2 drops 5.63% -> 2.81%,
       level 3 3.75% -> 1.88%. A hero facing two enemies on the SAME side can
       answer both; caught between two it can only face one, so the other hits it
       for free. The lifetime allowance is deliberately NOT scaled: an enemy still
       gets its full 45%/30%, it just needs twice the blows to spend it. Being
       surrounded buys time, it does not weaken the enemies.
    0b. SURROUNDED CEILING. While 3+ enemies fight it from both sides, the blow is
       additionally capped at LevelBattleRules.SurroundedDamagePerHit (1% of max).
       Level 3: 3.75% normal -> 1.88% flanked -> 1.00% surrounded.
       A CEILING, NOT A SET VALUE. At level 5 the halved blow is already 0.94%, and
       setting 1% there would make five attackers hurt MORE than two. Taking the
       smaller keeps the progression in one direction.
       It reads the count ONCE and shares it with the halving above - reading both
       properties here would repeat the whole walk per blow and could disagree if a
       unit moved between the two reads.
    1. no single blow above budget / 8 of maximum HP (level 2: 5.63%, level 3: 3.75%);
    2. each enemy has a lifetime allowance and stops mattering once spent;
    3. the END-OF-BATTLE reserve only: 1 - budget x enemyCount (10% at two or
       three enemies). It exists so a fully-spent army cannot kill the protected
       hero, nothing more.

    PER-BLOW TABLE, computed from the live constants 2026-09-12:
       level 1 (1 enemy, 95%)  11.88% normal   5.94% flanked      n/a surrounded
       level 2 (2, 45%)         5.63%          2.81%              n/a
       level 3 (3, 30%)         3.75%          1.88%           1.00%   (31 blows)
       level 4 (4, 20%)         2.50%          1.25%           1.00%   (20 blows)
       level 5 (5, 15%)         1.88%          0.94%           0.94%   (16 blows)
    The last column is how many blows ONE enemy then needs to spend its lifetime
    allowance - the deliberate consequence of not scaling the allowance.

    IT IS DELIBERATELY NOT STAGED. A staged version was tried - unlocking one
    enemy's budget per enemy still standing, so the hero would still be above half
    when the first died. Arash rejected it: each enemy's allowance is ITS OWN, and
    two enemies striking at once may spend both. In level 3 that is 30% + 30% =
    60%, taking the hero to 40% while the third walks on the base, rather than
    freezing it at a 70% floor. The per-enemy allowance is the real limit.

  ScriptTutorialBlow(target, isEnemy, hits)
    LEVELS 1-3 ONLY. The exchange is scripted so it LOOKS like a fight: an enemy
    loses exactly a quarter of its maximum per hit and dies on the fourth, and a
    hero loses a flat 7%. Both are SET, not capped, so the tutorial reads the same
    whatever the units were authored at. The killing blow is taken from currentHP
    so float drift can never leave a sliver of health behind.
    The champion's budget still applies on top, so it can never be worn past its
    reserve however long the fight runs.

    (RushKill, which let a lone outnumbered hero kill in two, was REMOVED the same
    day - superseded by the four-hit script plus the enemy speed reduction.)

  HasLivingDefenders(gate)                                              [static]
    A defended base cannot be bypassed by an attacker walking past the battle.

HOW IT CONNECTS
  - EnemySpawner             creates it, calls Prepare and RegisterEnemy
  - PlayerWaveManager        calls CalibrateHero per spawned hero
  - PlayerStats/EnemyStats   route damage through AdjustIncomingDamage, passing the
                             attacker (EnemyManager / PlayerManager respectively)
  - PlayerGateStats/EnemyGateStats   call HasLivingDefenders
  - LevelGameManager         skips mutual-wipe auto-defeat while IsPrepared

NOTES
  - REPLACED 2026-09-11 and must not come back: a last-survivor 1%-HP floor that
    triggered at the end of a fight instead of being budgeted before it, and a
    damage reduction hard-coded to one specific level and hero count.
  - The universal 25%-of-max-HP ceiling was briefly removed the same day, restored
    here as MinHitsToKillAnyone, found STILL not to hold in play, and finally moved
    down into CharacterStats where it cannot be bypassed. It was never the thing
    that made the game feel staged - the forced outcome and the last-survivor floor
    were. Do not remove it and do not move it back up here.
  - WHY TEAM-TOTAL NORMALISATION. Scaling every hero to an identical CP made the
    roster mathematically tied, so "the strongest hero" did not exist and this
    model had nothing to protect. Normalising the total keeps Player CP exactly on
    the workbook figure AND preserves each hero's share of it, so upgrades and
    archetypes still rank against each other.
  - Individual heroes now VARY around the per-hero reference rather than sitting on
    it. heroCount x PerHeroCP is a team figure, not a per-unit promise.
  - The champion fights whoever it is nearest, exactly like any other hero - there
    is no assigned duel. The budget is sized against the TOTAL enemy count, so it
    survives no matter which enemies it ends up facing or in what order.
  - Prepare throws ArgumentException internally and catches it; CalibrateHero does
    NOT, and PlayerWaveManager calls it outside any try. It no longer scales on
    that path unless a battle is already prepared, so the throw is much harder to
    reach, but a hero with zero CP reaching ScaleToCP would still surface there.
