PURPOSE
Stage 1-5 runtime CP calibration and hit-only tutorial assistance. Replaces the
rejected timed-budget implementation. Never updates HP on a timer, remotely
kills units, stops waves, or calls a gate damage/destruction method.

FIELDS
Instance is scene-scoped and cleared on destruction. PlayerCP/EnemyCP report
reference totals. PlayerShouldWin is the required match-count outcome, not an
observed result. BattleResult is labeled Expected. HeroCount and Level determine
outnumbered assistance; registered health components determine membership.

FLOW
PlayerWaveManager calibrates each hero to 100 CP at stage 1 or 125 at stages 2-5.
EnemySpawner waits for releases, then Prepare validates totals and computes the
enemy normalization. RegisterEnemy scales ATK and HP by sqrt(target/rawCP),
preserving archetype differences. Enemy totals: 80,100,115,400,650.
Physical PlayerStats/EnemyStats damage calls AdjustIncomingDamage. Blanket winner
armor and the boosted damage against losers have been removed. Both armies take
ordinary damage, capped at 25% of the struck unit's maximum health per hit.
A hit counter also prevents death before the fourth positive hit. Only the winning
side's LAST living unit has a 1% HP safety floor. For stage 5's four-hero Slow Loss,
heroes take 60% incoming damage to give them time to inflict casualties.
No other target's HP changes on that hit. HeroDeaths/EnemyDeaths and
FewestHitsBeforeDeath expose casualty/hit telemetry for real-scene verification.
HasLivingDefenders shields a base until its defending army has been defeated.
Afterward weapon hits damage the base normally. Gate events decide results.

NOTES
Tutorial assistance is separate from nominal CP, not a claimed predictive formula.
Dead/missing attackers or stuck AI can still stall siege; never auto-resolve.
No new mechanics apply at stage 6 onward. Shared prefab/SO stats are not modified.
