PURPOSE
-------
The glass capsule a hero stands in for the moment it appears on a deploy stage.
Rebuilt 1:1 from Arash's mockup
  Assets/Arts/GamePlay Assets/GameplayARTS/GameplayArts/Stage VFX/
      Hero Appearance animation/Preview_mockup.png
using ONLY the sprites in that folder (Main Glow, Stars_001-004,
Star-Tail_001/003/004). Arash's spec:
  - width  = exactly the stage's top-cap circle;
  - bottom = on the cap's front rim, never below the stage;
  - top    = middle of the hero's head;
  - 4 stars appear at the mockup spots (scattered, one per slot) as BARE stars;
    as they climb straight up they grow the tail hanging under them. Star 2
    (the low one) has no tail.
  - (pass 6, 2026-10-05) the glow moves like the LANDING PILLAR at the start
    of Assets/Arts/Reference videos/VFX ANIM/IMG_4311.MP4 (0.25-0.6 s, read
    at 30 fps): a column anchored at the feet SHOOTS UP from a stub to full
    height in ~0.12 s while fading in. (pass 7) It does NOT then stand still:
    it lifts off the cap and travels up like a flame - top outrunning the
    bottom so it stretches, thinning, flickering, swaying - fading on the
    way. ~0.95 s.
    (pass 8) Arash: it went far too high, and it still read as one block
    shifted up. Climb cut to riseBottom 0.25 / riseTop 0.6 (was 1.1 / 1.8),
    and the LOWER PARTS FADE FIRST: CapsuleRiseFade.shader fades everything
    below a soft, wobbling line that climbs from the bottom as it rises -
    alpha only, colour untouched.
    Pass 5 (a CapsuleFlame shader revealing the glow bottom -> top and
    burning it out) was REJECTED by Arash and deleted - do not bring it back.
  - !! COLOUR IS ARASH'S. Never tint, whiten, recolour his layers or push
    them above their own alpha. Motion = the glow's height/width + alpha only;
    the sliced rounded bottom always stays on the rim.
Replaced the 2026-10-04 particle version (VFXKit stage_capsule), which was too
wide, too tall, dipped below the stage, and split stars from their tails.

FIELDS
------
  glowSprite            Main Glow (80 x 111 px - the mockup's own glow size).
  stars[]               StarSlot: label, star sprite, tail sprite (empty = no
                        tail), mockupPos, delay.
      mockupPos is in MOCKUP PX from the capsule's bottom-centre (x right,
      y up). Measured off the mockup (glow box x315-394, y773-884):
        Star 1 (left)          (-34.5,  97.6)  tail 001   delay 0.00
        Star 2 (low, no tail)  (-20.1,  21.2)  -          delay 0.05
        Star 3 (top right)     ( 10.7, 119.3)  tail 003   delay 0.03
        Star 4 (mid right)     ( 29.6,  71.0)  tail 004   delay 0.08
      Cross-checked on a second hero in the mockup: same offsets within 1 px.
      delay = added to the moment the pillar reaches that star, so the four
      never move in lockstep.
  rimFromCapTop         56/82 - the cap's front rim as a share of the cap
                        sprite's height from its top (Top.png row 56 of 82).
  headCut               0.2 - capsule top = heroTop - 0.2 * heroHeight
                        (measured on the mockup = middle of the head).
  heightStretchClamp    (0.75, 1.35) x the mockup proportions.
  capEquatorFromTop     29/82 - the cap ellipse's widest row. (rim - equator) x cap
                        height = depth of the half-ellipse the capsule's bottom follows.
  glowCurvePx           22 - depth of Main Glow's rounded bottom (row 88 at the edge ->
                        110 at the centre). Main Glow has a 23 px BOTTOM slice border.
  shadowWidthOfCap      0.57 - the hero's shadow width during the stand, as a share
                        of the cap width (mockup: 46 px of 80).
  shadowRestoreWindow   (0.4, 0.55) s - the shadow eases back to its own size
                        around the hero's leap (hold 0.5 / 0.6), NOT at the end
                        of the effect, which outlives the stand.
  Pillar (reference timing):
  shootUp 0.14          stub -> overshoot height, EaseOutCubic, alpha in.
  startHeight 0.15      the stub, as a share of full height.
  overshoot 1.08        peak of the shoot-up; settles to 1.0 over another shootUp.
  flicker 0.06          +/- share of height the top flickers while standing ...
  flickerRate 14        ... changes per second (2 Perlin rates, random seed).
  riseStart 0.22        lift-off: the pillar starts travelling up.
  riseBottom 0.4        the BOTTOM climbs this many full heights by `end` ...
  riseTop 0.95          ... the TOP this many - so it stretches (not rigid).
                        Ease-in. History: 1.1 / 1.8 "goes up far too much" ->
                        0.25 / 0.6 -> 0.4 / 0.95 (pass 9: Arash circled where
                        the last wisp should reach - a little above the head
                        - before it vanishes).
  riseFadeMaterial      capsule_rise_fade.mat (Blasty/CapsuleRiseFade, prefab
                        folder). Empty = the pillar fades evenly.
  cutRange (-0.55, 1)   the bottom-first fade line (uv.y) from riseStart to end.
  riseThin 0.75         width it thins to as it rises.
  widthFlicker 0.06     width flicker while rising.
  sway 0.05             side sway while rising (share of cap width).
  fadeStart 0.72        whole-pillar fade-out starts (late, so the last wisp
                        stays visible while it climbs) ...
  end 0.95              ... and ends; Destroy.
  starPop 0.12, starHold 0.08, starRiseTime 0.5, starRiseMockupPx 60,
  tailFadeIn 0.1, starHoldShare 0.5 (share of a star's climb at full opacity).

FLOW
----
  Play(capBounds, heroTop, heroHeight, sortingLayerId, sortingOrder, shadow = null)
    1. Moves itself to the cap's front rim (centre x of the cap).
    2. sx = cap width / 0.80 (glow sprite width) - the uniform mockup scale.
       sy = the height that reaches mid-head, clamped around sx.
       glowYScale = cap half-ellipse depth / glow curve depth, so the glow's
       rounded bottom is EXACTLY the cap's front rim (Arash's red arc). The
       first version squashed the whole sprite by the height fit, flattening the
       curve so the bottom read as square.
    3. TakeShadow (the hero's "Shadow" SpriteRenderer) - Arash: the shadow must
       always be UNDER the effect, never on top of it:
         - remembers scale, local position, sorting layer + order;
         - shrinks it to the mockup size (shadowWidthOfCap x cap width);
         - LIFTS it just enough that its whole ellipse sits inside the glow's
           rounded bottom (bottom edge on the glow curve at the shadow's
           half-width, +0.01). The hero's feet stand on the rim, so at its own
           spot the lower half of the shadow hung out below the capsule, over
           the rim, and read as drawn on top of the effect (pass 4);
         - forces it to the hero's sorting layer at min(own order, hero - 1),
           so it is below the hero AND the glow/stars/tails by order, not by
           luck. (All hero prefabs: shadow 0, body 1, glow 2 - already below,
           now guaranteed.)
    4. Build(): creates at runtime
         Glow/Main Glow         order +1, drawMode SLICED, riseFadeMaterial. Glow root scaled (sx, glowYScale);
                                the sprite's size.y carries the height, so only
                                the straight column above the curve stretches.
                                Root on the rim, sprite above it. Random
                                flicker seed.
         <label>/Tail           order +2, top on the star's centre
         <label>/Star           order +3
       Each star root is scaled by sx only, so stars and tails never squash
       even when the glow is stretched; its resting spot uses sx / sy.
  Update/Apply (t = seconds since Play; Destroy(gameObject) at `end`):
    glow   height (share of full):
             t < shootUp : lerp(startHeight, overshoot, EaseOutCubic(t/shootUp))
             after       : lerp(overshoot, 1, EaseOutCubic(...)) settling over
                           another shootUp, x (1 +/- flicker) Perlin
             x (1 + (riseTop - riseBottom) * e)   e = p^2,
                           p = (t-riseStart)/(end-riseStart)
           lift   glow root y = riseBottom * fullWorldH * e  (bottom leaves the rim)
           width  sx * lerp(1, riseThin, e) * (1 +/- widthFlicker Perlin)
           sway   glow root x = +/- sway * cap width * p (Perlin)
           alpha  saturate(2.5 * t/shootUp) * (1 - f^2), f = (t-fadeStart)/(end-fadeStart)
           bottom-first fade (MaterialPropertyBlock on the glow):
                  _Cut = lerp(cutRange.x, cutRange.y, p), _FlameTime = t + seed.
                  CapsuleRiseFade.shader: alpha *= smoothstep(edge, edge + _Soft
                  (0.45), uv.y), edge = _Cut + noise wobble (_Wobble 0.12,
                  streaming up) - so the lower part breaks up and fades first,
                  the top last. Output rgb = sprite rgb; alpha <= sprite alpha.
           Height goes into the SLICED size.y, so the rounded bottom never
           distorts (on the rim until lift-off, then rising with the column).
    stars  starIgnite[i] = ShootTimeAt(mockupPos.y / 111) + delay
           (inverse of the shoot-up's EaseOutCubic) - each pops in BARE
           (EaseOutBack scale) when the pillar reaches it, holds starHold, then
           shoots up starRiseMockupPx * k^2 over starRiseTime; its tail fades
           in over tailFadeIn once moving; star + tail fade out over the last
           (1 - starHoldShare) of the climb.
    shadow   small + lifted from t = 0; scale and position eased back over
             shadowRestoreWindow; at its end RestoreShadow() puts back scale,
             position, layer and order in one go. OnDestroy calls
             RestoreShadow() too, if the capsule dies early.
  PreviewAt(time)  EDITOR ONLY: stops Update and freezes the pose at `time`
                   (used for edit-mode captures).

HOW IT CONNECTS
---------------
  - PlayerWaveManager.gateArrivalVfx (LevelTemplate.prefab) ->
    Assets/VFXKit/Effects/hero_arrival_capsule/hero_arrival_capsule.prefab.
  - PlayerWaveManager.PlayGateArrivalVfx(pm, gate) finds the cap (the gate's
    highest-order SpriteRenderer = Top_0) and the hero's body bounds (health
    bars skipped), instantiates the prefab and calls Play.
  - The capsule is unparented: if the hero leaves the stage before `end`,
    the pillar finishes fading on the empty stage.

NOTES
-----
  - The new sprites were imported as Multiple, and Unity auto-sliced Main Glow
    into two halves at its dark centre seam (only the left 41 px drew). All 8
    were switched to Single / Uncompressed / no mips on 2026-10-05. If the art
    is re-imported, keep them Single - and keep Main Glow's 23 px bottom border.
  - Since 2026-10-05 (PlayerWaveManager.GetStageStandPoint) heroes stand in the
    CENTRE of the cap's circle, so the shadow already sits inside the glass and
    TakeShadow's lift normally comes out 0 - it stays as a guard.
  - The hero "Shadow" (CH Shadow, 0.74 wide) is ~87% of the cap and covered the
    whole stage; it is shrunk ONLY for the stand, battlefield shadows unchanged.
  - In-game the hero stands lower on the stage than in the mockup (feet on the
    rim, not on the cap), so the mid-head rule makes the capsule ~25% shorter
    than the mockup's proportions (it hits the 0.75 clamp on stage 1-1).
  - Verified in edit mode only (frozen frames via PreviewAt; pass 3 at
    0.1 / 0.45 / 0.7 / 0.95 s on a temp stage built from Top/Mid/Bot.png).
    NOT play-tested.
