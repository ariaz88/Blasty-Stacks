PURPOSE
2026-09-11: an enemy yields recovery positioning to a hero currently targeting it.
Normal locomotion and recovery therefore agree that the enemy stays planted;
the recovery mover can no longer pull it toward the hero after locomotion stops.

Stage 1-5 melee contact recovery for current player/enemy prefabs. Added at runtime
by CPBattleController; does not modify shared prefab assets or later stages.

FLOW
After 2.5 seconds without its own weapon hitting its target, a nearby melee unit
tries a standoff point to one side, between attack animations. Movement uses its
Rigidbody2D and live movement speed, with a bounded 1.1-second attempt and retry
delay. Retries vary the SLOT (a step up or down the same flank), never the side,
and every destination comes from MeleeEngagement.StandPoint, so it is always a
legal fighting spot inside normal melee range. Managers temporarily suspend attack
-state ticks during the physical sidestep, and EnemyLocoMotion suspends MOVEMENT.
Weapon colliders report actual contacts; a successful hit resets the missed-contact
timer. Working fights are left alone. Gates are not recovery targets.

FIELDS
IsRepositioning gates the normal manager attack tick AND EnemyLocoMotion.FixedUpdate.
RepositionCount is runtime diagnostic telemetry. Cached player/enemy/health/body/
target references scope work to this fighter. No HP, damage, CP, gate-result or
teleport operations occur here.

Awake also calls EnemyLocoMotion.BindContactRecovery(this). That hand-off is
required, not cosmetic: this component is ADDED AT RUNTIME, long after
EnemyLocoMotion.Awake has run, so the locomotion's own GetComponent finds nothing
and it would never know to stand down.

NOTES
This is a local recovery step, not a general navigation/pathfinding replacement.
Recovery stops on death, lost/dead target, locked deployment, pause or battle end.

CHANGED 2026-09-11, two fixes to the retry destination:
  - It used to be `target.position + (side * radius, 0)` with a hand-rolled radius
    that shrank to 0.55 * range on every third attempt. That put the unit at the
    target's EXACT Y and deliberately walked it deeper INTO the body it was meant
    to be standing beside - which is what made the two sprites overlap, usually
    with the enemy's head drawn over the hero.
  - It used to flip the SIDE on odd attempts (`if ((attempts & 1) != 0) side =
    -side;`), marching the unit straight through its target to the far side. A
    unit standing on the far side of something that is also trying to stand on ITS
    far side is the one arrangement with no solution, and the pair slides sideways
    together. See MeleeEngagement.StandPoint.

With the lateral approach now built into normal locomotion for BOTH factions, this
component is a fallback for fights that still fail to connect rather than the main
answer to vertical alignment. It is still only added on stages 1-5; stage 6+ relies
on the locomotion fix alone.
