PURPOSE
Explicit campaign-stage rules for stages 1-5 only. Spreadsheet Level means the
scene stage number, not the LevelManager chapter number.

FLOW
ResolveLevel prioritizes the _Stage_N scene suffix, then config/saved fallback.
TotalPairs/HeroesForMatch/TotalHeroes describe the five deployment sequences.
FirstWinningMatch is 1,1,1,3,4. ReferenceEnemyCP is 80,100,115,400,650.
Stages beyond five are excluded. Later-stage generalization is not implemented.

NOTES
Stage 5 has SIX pairs, as shown in the screenshot. Fixed enemy count equals stage
number for stages 1-5. Full-board hero totals are 3,4,5,8,12.
