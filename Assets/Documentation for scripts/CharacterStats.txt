================================================================================
  CharacterStats.cs
  Path : Assets/Scripts/Combat/Shared/CharacterStats.cs
  Type : MonoBehaviour  (base class of PlayerStats and EnemyStats)
  Layer: Combat / Shared
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
The health a unit carries, and - since 2026-09-11 - the single place the FOUR-HIT
RULE is enforced: nobody, hero or enemy, at any stage, dies in fewer than four
weapon blows.

FIELDS
------
  currentHP     current health
  maxHealth     maximum health (default 100; spawners overwrite it from unitStats)
  IsAlive       currentHP > 0
  hitsTaken     private, [NonSerialized]. Blows this unit has actually absorbed.
  HitsTaken     read-only view of the above

METHODS
-------
  Init(maxHP)
      Sets currentHP, floored at 1.

  ClampIncomingBlow(damage) -> float
      Call LAST on every damage path. Three mechanisms:
        1. CEILING of maxHealth / HitsToKillMe. The divisor is THIS unit's own
           number from the toughness band, not a constant - see HitsToKillMe.
        2. FLOOR of maxHealth / MaxHitsToKillAnyone, so a very soft blow is lifted
           and no duel drags past about eleven exchanges. Suppressed by passing
           allowFloor:false - PlayerStats does this for the PROTECTED hero.
           The floor exists to lift blows that are weak because of STATS; it must
           never lift one a design budget deliberately lowered. It did, and the
           champion's 45%/8 = 5.6% blows were raised to maxHP/11 = 9.1%, spending
           the whole allowance in five hits instead of eight, then freezing on the
           reserve. Reported from a level 2 playthrough as "10% per hit, not 5%".
        3. LETHALITY GUARD - before the eighth blow the unit cannot be reduced past
           a sliver (0.1% of max). Catches float drift and damage arriving outside
           the normal weapon path.
      Increments hitsTaken only when damage is actually positive, so a blow
      zeroed upstream (pause, battle not running) does not consume a hit.

  Defense                                                          [protected]
      Overridden by PlayerStats and EnemyStats to read the LIVE stat block, so an
      upgrade that raises defence immediately moves the unit up the band.

  HitsToKillMe -> int
      LEVELS 1-3: a FLAT MinHitsToKillAnyone (8) for everyone, whatever its
      defence, because those levels run a scripted exchange whose kill must land
      on the eighth blow. Letting the band apply there made a defence-65 enemy
      take 10 and a defence-78 one take 11 - reported from play.
      LEVELS 4-5: LevelBattleRules.HitsToKill(Defense) - 8 at no defence rising to
      11 at defence 80+. On the current roster: 25 -> 9, 52/65 -> 10, 78 -> 11.
      The switch is CPBattleController.IsTutorialExchange.

  ResetHitHistory()
      Clears hitsTaken and the attacker table, for a revived or re-pooled unit.

  ReportDeath()
      Logs ONE line per death: total blows absorbed, the PER-ATTACKER breakdown
      (name x count), the number the band required, and OK / RULE BROKEN. When
      more than one attacker landed blows it also prints the smallest of those
      counts - the number a viewer watching only one duel would have counted.
      Unconditional on purpose. It exists to settle "it died in four hits" with
      data instead of another round of guessing from a video - and the usual
      answer is that several attackers shared the kill, so each one only swung
      four times while the unit really did absorb eight.
      The breakdown was added 2026-09-12 after a level 4 recording: the enemy
      that "died in four blows" is logged as ten from two attackers, the second
      being a hero fighting a DIFFERENT enemy whose weapon box reached across.

HOW IT CONNECTS
---------------
  - PlayerStats.ApplyDamageToPlayer and EnemyStats.ApplyDamageToEnemy both call
    ClampIncomingBlow AFTER CPBattleController.AdjustIncomingDamage.
  - LevelBattleRules.HitsToKill / MinHitsToKillAnyone / MaxHitsToKillAnyone /
    DefenseForMaxHits supply the band.
  - CPBattleController still owns the CHAMPION budget and the levels 1-3 script;
    this class owns only the universal floor.

NOTES
-----
  - WHY THE RULE LIVES HERE AND NOT IN CPBattleController. It was implemented
    there first and DID NOT HOLD in play - a level 4 enemy still died in two hits.
    AdjustIncomingDamage returns the damage untouched on several paths that are
    invisible at runtime: no battle prepared, the unit missing from the registered
    hero/enemy sets, a stale static Instance left by a previous battle, or a scene
    mismatch. Every one of those silently skipped the ceiling. Down here the unit
    clamps its own incoming damage, so the rule holds with no CP battle in the
    scene at all - which is also what makes it true for stage 6+.
  - ORDER MATTERS. The levels 1-3 script takes its killing blow from currentHP on
    the defender's final hit; the guard only binds while hitsTaken < 8, and that
    script now uses target.HitsToKillMe rather than its own constant, so the kill
    passes through instead of being clipped to a sliver.
  - This is a UNIVERSAL rule now, not a stages 1-5 one. If a later design wants
    fast kills somewhere, gate it here rather than reintroducing a bypass upstream.
  - WHY THE CEILING IS PER-DEFENDER. A single fixed ceiling (maxHealth/8) was tried
    and measured over the whole roster: 240 of 240 matchups landed on EXACTLY 8
    hits. After CP normalisation every attacker comfortably clears a flat 12.5%
    blow, so the ceiling bound every time and all variety collapsed. Sizing it from
    the defender's own defence is what restores 8/9/10/11.
  - Hero variety is currently limited by the ROSTER, not by this code: every
    deployed hero is authored at defence 25, so they all land on 9. Give heroes
    distinct defence values and they spread across the band immediately.
  - THE BAND COUNTS TOTAL BLOWS, NOT BLOWS PER ATTACKER. Two heroes on one enemy
    still kill it in eight, so each of them swings only four times - which looks
    exactly like a broken rule when you watch a single hero. Measured: 8 total /
    4 per hero / rule satisfied. If the intent is that a fight should last eight
    EXCHANGES no matter how many heroes join, this is the line to change.
  - THE SECOND ATTACKER IS OFTEN NOT FIGHTING THE VICTIM AT ALL. Weapon boxes used
    to be ~1.5 world units wide against a 0.45-wide body, and PlayerDamageCollider
    / EnemyDamageCollider damage EVERY unit they overlap with no check against the
    attacker's currentTarget. In a packed 4v4 line a hero therefore hit its own
    target AND its neighbours. Narrowed to 0.8 across 17 prefabs on 2026-09-12,
    which makes the extra victim much rarer but is NOT a guarantee - only filtering
    a swing to currentTarget would be. Check the ReportDeath breakdown before
    concluding the clamp is at fault.
